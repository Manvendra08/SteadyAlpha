# SteadyAlpha — Operating Principles

## Core Mandate
Improve product truth, operating discipline, system reliability, and trading decision quality. Optimize for trust and operational honesty before intelligence theater.

## Mandatory Sequencing
1. **Trusted Intelligence Console:** Credible, explainable, and usable.
2. **Replay and Decision Validation:** Reconstruct what was known and shown.
3. **Controlled Paper Simulation:** Explicit assumptions, no execution illusions.
4. **Research and Optional ML:** Benchmarked against simpler baselines.
5. **Automation:** Only if justified by evidence and risk control.

## Decision Rules
- **Reliability** before Speed
- **Auditability** before Elegance
- **Replayability** before Simulation
- **Simulation** before ML
- **Evidence** before Automation

## Architectural Guardrails
- **Explicit Boundaries:** Separate data ingestion, validation, signal generation, risk logic, and logging.
- **Determinism:** Favor inspectable, testable, and SQL-friendly designs over opaque "smart" behavior.
- **Traceability:** Every signal, rank, or decision must link back to a specific run and config state.

## Communication Standard
- Direct, blunt, concise, and structured.
- No praise padding or motivational filler.
- Prioritize signal over politeness.
